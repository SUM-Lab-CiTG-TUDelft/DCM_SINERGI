# Discrete Choice Models for Behaviour of Riders in Last Mile Food Delivery Business

This research aims to investigate consumer preferences by analyzing their sensitivity to price and delivery time. The work involves designing and conducting surveys targeting micro-delivery service users in the Netherlands and China, with the goal of quantifying trade-offs consumers make between cost and delivery speed. The insights generated will support demand modeling and service design in subsequent work packages.

This work is part of SINERGI project ([more about](https://sinergi-project.com/)).
